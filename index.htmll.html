To My Babyyy, My Favourite Person 🤍

For the boy who unknowingly became one of the most beautiful parts of my life.

Hi, my babyyyy. 🥹🤍

If you’re reading this, I hope you know how much you mean to me. I don’t know how to put all my feelings into words, but I’ll try, because you deserve to know just how special you are to me.

Out of all the people in this world, I’m so glad my heart found you.

Baby, maybe we’re still young, and maybe we still have so much to learn about love, life and each other. But one thing I know is that what I feel for you is something I truly treasure. I don’t just want you for the happy moments, for the cute pictures, or for the butterflies you give me. I want to be there for the ordinary days too.

I want to know how your day went. I want to hear about the things that make you happy, the things that stress you out, and even the little things you think nobody would care about. I want to be someone you can come to when the world feels too heavy. You don’t always have to be strong with me, babyyy. You can be tired, quiet, emotional or lost sometimes. You don’t have to pretend you’re okay just to make me happy.

You are loved, even on the days when you don’t feel like your best self.

And baby, if one day we have a difficult day, if we misunderstand each other or if life gets a little harder than usual, I hope we remember why we chose each other. I hope we learn to communicate instead of giving up, to understand instead of assuming, and to fix things together instead of letting our pride get in the way.

I can’t promise that every day will be perfect, because we’re only human. But I hope we can keep choosing honesty, kindness, patience and effort. I want our love to be more than sweet words. I want it to be something we show through the way we treat each other, even when things aren’t easy.

Sometimes I wonder how one person can make such a difference in my day just by texting me, calling me, or simply being there. And then I realise it’s you, babyyy. The person whose voice can make me feel calmer, whose little messages can make me smile at my phone, and whose presence makes even the simplest moments feel special.

You don’t have to be perfect to be my favourite person. You just have to be you.

Thank you for being part of my life, for the little moments we’ve shared, and for the memories we’re still going to make. I hope we get to look back one day and laugh about how we started, how shy or silly we were, and how much we’ve grown together.

And if life allows us to go far together, I hope we get to experience so many more things side by side. More late-night conversations, more silly jokes, more hugs after a long day, more celebrations, more lessons, and more little moments that become our favourite memories.

I don’t know exactly what the future holds, baby. But I know that when I think about the kind of love I want to build, I hope it’s a love where we both feel safe, respected, appreciated and understood.

So, my babyyy, if you ever come back to this little website on a day when you’re tired, stressed, or wondering whether someone truly cares about you, please read these words again.

Somewhere in this world, there is a girl who is so happy that she got to know you, who is grateful that you became part of her story, and who hopes you never forget how much your existence matters.

And that girl is me, your sayang. 🥹🫶🏻

I love you, babyyy. Not just for the way you make me feel, but for the person you are, the person you’re becoming, and all the little things that make you you.

No matter how many times you visit this website, I hope you remember this one thing:

You are not just someone I love. You are someone I hope to keep learning, growing and building beautiful memories with.

With all my love,

Your sayang, always cheering for you. 🤍🌷
